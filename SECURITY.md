# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes may be provided for the latest released major version.
Pre-release, development, and older major versions are unsupported unless
Grootan Technologies Pvt Ltd states otherwise.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue, discussion, or pull request. Email suspected
vulnerabilities privately to `platform-engineering@grootan.com`.

## Safe reporting and disclosure

- Stop testing immediately if it could access data without authorization,
  disrupt a service, degrade availability, or affect a third party.
- Test only systems and accounts you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
- Do not exfiltrate, retain, alter, or destroy data.
- Keep the report confidential until Grootan Technologies Pvt Ltd agrees that
  coordinated disclosure is appropriate.
- Submission does not guarantee acknowledgement, investigation, remediation,
  disclosure, or a particular response timeline.
